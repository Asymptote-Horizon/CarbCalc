How the estimate
is calculated.

01 / PETROLDaily km ÷ km per litre ÷ occupants × 2.33

Uses 2.33 kg CO₂ per litre of petrol, as given in the supplied presentation script.

02 / ELECTRICITY(Monthly kWh ÷ 30 ÷ occupants) × 0.82

Uses 0.82 kg CO₂ per kWh, as given in the supplied presentation script.

03 / WASTEDaily unsegregated waste (kg) × 0.63

Uses 0.63 kg CO₂ per kg of waste, as given in the supplied presentation script.

TOTAL DAILY ESTIMATEPetrol + electricity + waste

These are project starter factors, not universal constants. The model excludes food, flights, purchases, public-transport lifecycle emissions, and other sources not included in the supplied formulas. The three status bands are project-defined display bands, not official standards.
